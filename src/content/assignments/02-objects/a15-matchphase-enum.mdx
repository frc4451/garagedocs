### Scenario

In [A05](/java/assignments/a05-match-phase-detector) a phase was a `String`, and every
question about it: when does it start, how much drive power is safe: meant another
`if` chain somewhere else. An enum lets each constant carry its own answers.

By the end, `phaseAt(75)` returns something that already knows it runs from 15 to 120,
allows full drive power, and calls itself "Teleop".

### What to build

`MatchPhase` with four constants, each carrying `startSeconds`,
`endSecondsExclusive`, `maxDriveScaling` and `label`. The constants and constructor are
written for you. You add three things:

  "<strong><code>OVER</code> runs to <code>Integer.MAX_VALUE</code>.</strong> That is what makes any large time land somewhere. Negative times still match nothing, which is why <code>phaseAt</code> needs the throw at the end rather than a fallback return."
