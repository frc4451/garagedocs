The Automation Badges are how FRC 4451's Automation Team tracks a student's progress from their
first meeting to a working robot. Each badge has a printed packet:
  - a list of tasks
  - a mentor signature with date
  - any required worksheets for the badge

The lessons on this site are the material behind the tasks, and the pages in this section list them
in the order the sheet signs them off. The intention is one badge per year; each badge is a flat
list of steps.

<RulesBox title="Recommendations">

- Especially in Badge 1, the student should ***hand-write*** all notes from the curriculum material.
- It is best practice for students to keep a notebook of their notes and observations, as well as
  any questions.
- Each section is intended to be completed within 30 to 60 minute chunks, meaning that progress can
  be made either in a single day or over the course of several sessions. The student is expected to
  pace themselves to avoid burn-out.

</RulesBox>

### How a Badge Is Signed Off

Badge 3 uses a diagnostic, an independent mastery attempt at 100% after instruction and corrections,
and a separate equivalent final check at 100%. Its practical and leadership evidence is reviewed
separately. See [Badge 3 Written Checks](/badges/badge-3-written-checks).


Badges 1 and 2 open and close with the same short pretest ([Badge 1](/badges/badge-1-pretest),
[Badge 2](/badges/badge-2-pretest)). The first sitting is scored but not graded; the second is
graded, so the badge measures its own effect. Every other task on a sheet has a **done when** the
mentor can check without taking the student's word for it: a repository the mentor can open, a
simulation running on the student's laptop, a log file, a demonstration on the practice field. A
mentor signs a row after seeing the evidence, not after hearing about it.

Badge 1 work lives in two repositories the student owns: `<yourname>Badge01` for Steps 2 through 5
(the Git rows and the Java assignments, set up in
[A00](/java/assignments/a00-practice-repository-setup)) and a Kit Bot repository created when the
[Kit Bot](/kit-bot) lessons start. Badge 2 work lives in one repository, `<yourname>Badge02`,
holding Java practice and three robot projects: `Badge2Bench` for every mechanism and the physical
fixture, `Badge2KitBot` for PathPlanner on a differential drivetrain, and `Badge2Swerve` for the
holonomic drivetrain, vision and the capstone. Every later artifact has a home a mentor can reach.
