The Automation Badges are how FRC 4451's Automation Team tracks a student's progress from their first meeting to a working robot.
Each badge has a printed packet:
  - a list of tasks
  - a mentor signature with date
  - any required worksheets for the badge

The lessons on this site are the material behind the tasks, and the pages in this section list them in the order the sheet signs them off.
The intention is one badge per year; each badge is a flat list of steps.

<RulesBox title="Recommendations">

- Especially in Badge 1, you will be recommmended to ***hand-write*** any and all notes from the curriculum material.
- It is best practice for students to keep a notebook of their notes and observations, as well as any questions.
- Each section is intended to be completed within 30 to 60 minute chunks, meaning that progress can be made either in a single day or over the course of several sessions. The student is expected to pace themselves to avoid burn-out.

</RulesBox>

### How a badge is signed off

Each badge opens and closes with the same short pretest ([Badge 1](/badges/badge-1-pretest), [Badge 2](/badges/badge-2-pretest)). The first sitting is scored but not graded; the second is graded, so the badge measures its own effect. Every other task on a sheet has a **done when** the mentor can check without taking the student's word for it: a repository the mentor can open, a simulation running on the student's laptop, a log file, a demonstration on the practice field. A mentor signs a row after seeing the evidence, not after hearing about it.

Badge 1 work lives in two repositories the student owns: `<yourname>Badge01` for Steps 2 through 5 (the Git rows and the Java assignments, set up in [A00](/java/assignments/a00-practice-repository-setup)) and a Kit Bot repository created when the [Kit Bot](/kit-bot) lessons start. Badge 2 work lives in one repository, `<yourname>Badge02`, holding its two projects. Every later artifact has a home a mentor can reach.
