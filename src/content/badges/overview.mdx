The Automation Badges are how FRC 4451's Automation Team tracks a student's progress from their first meeting to a working robot. Each badge has a printed packet: a list of tasks, a mentor signature and a date next to each one, plus any required worksheets. The lessons on this site are the material behind the tasks, and the pages in this section put them in the order the sheet signs them off. One badge per year; each badge is a flat list of steps.

### How a badge is signed off

Badge 1 opens and closes with the same short [pretest](/badges/badge-1-pretest), scored but not graded, so the badge measures its own effect. Every other task on a sheet has a **done when** the mentor can check without taking the student's word for it: a repository the mentor can open, a simulation running on the student's laptop, a log file, a demonstration on the practice field. A mentor signs a row after seeing the evidence, not after hearing about it.

Student work lives in two repositories the student owns: the Practice repository from [A00](/java/assignments/a00-practice-repository-setup) and a Kit Bot repository created the same way when the [Kit Bot](/kit-bot) lessons start. Every later artifact has a home a mentor can reach.

### Where the lessons come from

Most badge pages link to the [Tools](/tools), [Java](/java), [Practice](/java/assignments) and [Kit Bot](/kit-bot) lessons to complete. Step 4 includes a classroom worksheet in the printed sign-off packet. When a lesson changes, the badge page links to the changed lesson.
