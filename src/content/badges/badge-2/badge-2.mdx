Badge 2 is the Automation Team's second-year badge. Its requirements are not settled, so this page holds what it is expected to certify and where its material will come from. Nothing on it is a sign-off row yet.

### Expected to certify

A second-year member who holds Badge 2 is expected to be able to read and change FRC 4451's competition robot code, not only a Kit Bot: the command-based structure as Cobra does it, AdvantageKit logging and replay, simulation before hardware, PID and feedforward tuning by hand, and the pose estimation the robot aims with.

### What exists today

The [FRC](/frc) section already carries the lessons this badge will list: [Kit Bot to Cobra](/frc/kit-bot-to-cobra) and the [Cobra Repository Tour](/frc/cobra-repository-tour), the [Command-Based Programming](/frc/introduction-to-command-based), [Logging and Replay](/frc/advantagekit) and [Simulation](/frc/simulation-first) groups, [Control Theory](/frc/pid-control) with the [Manual Tuning](/frc/manual-tuning-introduction) recipes and the [PID Tuning Practice](/frc/pid-tuning-practice-setup) sessions, and [Pose Estimation](/frc/introduction-to-pose-estimation). What is missing is the badge itself: which of those pages are required, in what order, and what the mentor checks.

### Before it is written

The badge follows the same shape as [Badge 1](/badges/badge-1): a pretest at the start and the end, numbered steps that list existing lessons with a *done when* per row, badge checks a mentor can see, and a sign-off sheet. When the mentors settle the outcomes, this page becomes the badge's *What It Certifies* page and the steps follow it in this list.
