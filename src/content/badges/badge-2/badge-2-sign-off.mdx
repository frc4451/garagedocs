Step 14 closes Badge 2 after the integrated capstone and successful replay. The student completes the post-test and reviews the accumulated evidence with a mentor. A passing written score does not replace the practical work.

## Required Sheet

Print the [Badge 2 requirements and sign-off sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19bYHl6gLpLtoIs9wSAsRVje0Jok5U5xWoD9A7rcnjWs/edit). It follows the step order and contains the numbered requirements, spaces for signatures and dates, both assessment scores, and the final review. The signed paper remains the badge record; the repository README links each requirement to evidence.

For Step 7, open the complete five-family, two-controller capability matrix and choose a cell to inspect its implementation, simulation, and test. For Step 8, choose one interlock to violate and verify the refusal and defined safe state in both a test and a log. For Step 10, inspect all three required routines and their logs, including the BLine starting-offset demonstration.

For Step 11, review the two-camera simulation and deliberate transform-error demonstration. For Step 12, trace a sensor value through the estimator, state object, derived fact, trigger, and command. For Step 13, demonstrate both swerve routines, an interruption, and successful replay with a newly added diagnostic output. An explanation of a replay failure helps diagnose it but does not complete the requirement.

The mentor also asks the student to name one library design they would change after using it and explain the tradeoff. A specific reason matters more than choosing a particular redesign.

## Cross-Step Questions

The student may use notes for these practical-review questions. Answers belong in the student's own words.

1. **Ownership:** A shooter command wants to change the intake roller directly because it knows the shooter is ready. Trace the owning subsystem and show how the readiness fact can influence an allowed command.
2. **Frames:** Vision observations are consistently displaced relative to the robot. Name the transform and frame evidence to check, then describe a test that distinguishes a sign error from using the inverse transform.
3. **Control:** The same mechanism responds differently with Talon FX and Talon FXS IO. Name two configuration, conversion, or simulation differences to inspect and the evidence that would confirm each.
4. **Autonomous:** Run from the drawn start and from an offset. Explain each follower's inputs, progress model, and recovery using the observed logs. Do not assume one follower must fail or recover identically in every case.
5. **Java and Tests:** Show a record, callback, or collection from the project. Explain who owns its data, when it is read, and one test that could detect a mistake in that behavior.

If an explanation needs work, the mentor reviews the relevant lesson and tries another example. Completed checks remain complete.

## Assessment and Signature

Use a fresh copy of the [ten-question assessment](/badges/badge-2-pretest). The student scores at least 18 of 20 without notes or internet. The mentor compares the first and final papers with the student after scoring the second sitting.

| # | Work | Done When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14.1 | Post-test | The final score is at least 18 of 20 and is recorded beside the initial score |
| 14.2 | Evidence review | Every numbered row in Steps 2–13 is signed and has its evidence in the README |
| 14.3 | Cross-step explanation | The student answers the five practical questions and explains one design improvement |
| 14.4 | Final signature | The mentor records the completion date after confirming the capstone and successful replay |
