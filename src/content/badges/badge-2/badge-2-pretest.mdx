Take the assessment before Step 2 and again after Step 13. The first sitting identifies starting knowledge; it has no passing mark. The final sitting requires at least 18 of 20 points.

## Printed Test

The mentor prints a fresh copy of the [Badge 2 pretest](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oHxe6Rt1QnBW95DJVc9To73P6bVHDBe-QlSQtFt8AYI/edit) for each sitting. The document contains ten questions and space for answers. Each question has two parts worth one point each. Several questions connect more than one topic.

The test is taken on paper without notes or internet. The mentor sets the available time and may clarify the wording without supplying answers. The student can write “not sure” or show partial reasoning. Some topics are new in Badge 2; missing them on the first attempt is expected.

## Scoring

Award one point for each correct part, for two points per question and twenty points total. The mentor uses the separate scoring guidance; answers are not included in the student document. Record both scores on the sign-off sheet. Keep the first paper for comparison after the post-test, and review missed answers after that second sitting.

If the final score is below 18, use the topic table to choose practice with the mentor before another attempt. Previously completed practical checks remain complete.
