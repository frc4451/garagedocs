Step 4 teaches how to combine true-or-false conditions and decide what follows from given facts. Work through the lesson in class, then complete the paper worksheet.

### Required work

Read [Boolean Logic](/java/fundamentals/java-boolean-logic), then complete the worksheet below.

### Boolean Logic worksheet

Open the [Boolean Logic worksheet in Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WFRUK9Oz_xbyb7OJYDED7Dt4KJtrxJgEF3IeMDeb1Sk/edit) and print a copy. Complete all five sections on paper without running code, then review and correct your answers with a mentor. Explain the modus ponens and modus tollens problems in your own words.

The worksheet covers five groups: statements, truth tables, equivalent expressions, if-then statements, and rules for drawing conclusions. Read the definitions beside each group before answering. You may use the lesson and your notes; do the reasoning without running code.

### Badge check

The mentor signs this step when:

1. Every worksheet section is complete and corrected.
2. The student can explain why the conclusion follows in the modus ponens problem.
3. The student can explain why the conclusion follows in the modus tollens problem.

### Programming Math after Badge 1

[Discrete Math Foundations](/java/fundamentals/java-discrete-math) expands this lesson into sets, relations, functions, counting, probability and graphs. Badge 1 does not require that full lesson. [Vector Math](/java/fundamentals/java-vector-math) and [Matrix Math](/java/fundamentals/java-matrix-math) are also later material.
