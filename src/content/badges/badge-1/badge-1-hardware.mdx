Step 7 puts the Step 6 code on a real Kit Bot. The subsystem design stays the same; `RobotContainer` already picks the real IO implementation when `RobotBase.isReal()` is true ([R12](/kit-bot/r12-real-vs-sim)). A mentor helps the student check the roboRIO image, the network, the driver controller's USB slot, and the test area before enabling. Hardware may need different directions or settings from the simulation.

### Rows

| # | Step | Where it is taught | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Image the roboRIO and set the team number | [R00 Part 2: Image the roboRIO](/kit-bot/r00-roborio-imaging) | The Driver Station reaches the roboRIO over USB and shows the correct team number and current image year |
| 7.2 | Update and configure the radio | [R00 Part 3: Configure the Robot Radio](/kit-bot/r00-radio-setup) | The radios have the current firmware and matching settings; the Driver Station has communications and `ping` reaches the roboRIO |
| 7.3 | Set CAN IDs and check the bus | [R12 Part 2: First Deploy](/kit-bot/r12-first-deploy#prepare-rhc2) | Every SPARK MAX appears once with the ID used in `Constants` |
| 7.4 | Deploy with the real IO | [R12 Part 2: First Deploy](/kit-bot/r12-first-deploy#deploy-the-real-branch) | *WPILib: Deploy Robot Code* succeeds and the console reports `IO: real` |
| 7.5 | Safety check before the first enable | [R12 Part 2: First Deploy](/kit-bot/r12-first-deploy#inspect-the-robot) | Robot on blocks, mechanism clear, and one person stationed at Driver Station **Disable** |
| 7.6 | Drive the real Kit Bot | [R11](/kit-bot/r11-teleop-drive) on hardware | Forward is forward and the turn direction is correct; the robot holds still inside the deadband |
| 7.7 | Launch on hardware | [R08 Part 2](/kit-bot/r08-launcher-io-part-2) on hardware | The bumpers intake and launch; on launch, the intake and launcher roller starts at once and the feeder waits through spin-up; AdvantageScope shows both motors |
| 7.8 | Autonomous on carpet | [R14](/kit-bot/r14-time-based-autonomous) on hardware | The routine chosen in Glass drives and stops within its timeout |

Rows 7.1 through 7.5 are one bring-up sequence (roboRIO image, radio, CAN inventory, safe deploy) that ends at the first motion in row 7.6. The mentor stays beside the student through the whole sequence, even when a row was practiced on another robot.

### Badge checks

<StepsBox title="Flash hardware">

1. The student images the roboRIO with the current year's image and sets the team number. The Driver Station shows it connected.
2. The student flashes the radio to the current firmware and configures it for the team number. The laptop joins the robot network and `ping` reaches the roboRIO.

</StepsBox>

<StepsBox title="Hardware demonstration">

1. With the robot on blocks and disabled, the student deploys and shows `IO: real`, then reviews the first action with the mentor before enabling.
2. Still on blocks, the student drives the wheels forward and reverse and turns, confirming direction and deadband.
3. The student disables before the robot comes off the blocks. With the mentor ready at Disable and the area clear, the student enables on carpet, drives a short course, intakes and launches fuel, and shows the launcher graph.
4. The student selects and runs one autonomous routine from Elastic. It stops on its own.
5. The student disables and explains one difference observed between the simulator and the robot (a direction that had to be inverted, a speed that differed, a value that was noisier).

</StepsBox>

The student performs each step. The mentor supervises hardware safety and stays ready at Disable during motion. Questions and practice come first; any unfinished check is repeated when the student is ready.

The student saves the three hardware artifacts listed in the [Kit Bot capstone](/kit-bot/rc-capstone#check-the-evidence-files), the labeled CAN-ID photo, the drive video, and the intake-and-launch side view, in the Kit Bot repository or linked from its README.

<RulesBox title="Stop and ask">

- The fix for a motor that spins the wrong way is in `Constants` or the IO implementation, never in swapping wires under power.
- If a SPARK MAX is missing from the REV Hardware Client, the student disables and checks its power, USB connection, firmware, and CAN wiring with the mentor. The missing device alone does not identify the cause.
- The Driver Station says *No Robot Code*: the deploy failed or the code crashed on start, and the console says which. Redeploying without reading it hides the cause.

</RulesBox>
