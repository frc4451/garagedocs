The student takes this test twice:
- Once in their first week, before any lesson (the pretest).
  - This sitting is _not_ graded. It is an evaluation of what the student does, and does not, know.
- Once after the capstone (the post-test).
  - This sitting _is_ graded. The student is expected to score 18 of 20 (90%) or higher on completing the Badge 1 curriculum.

### Pretest document

The mentor prints a fresh copy of the [Badge 1 pretest in Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rq0OwiinRpR1t8vgwaydea3KnQgzvDeNmqIgvBGcHwo/edit) for each attempt.
The document contains all ten questions and space for handwritten answers.

<RulesBox title="How it works">

- Ten questions on paper, without notes or internet. The student writes their name and the date at the top. The mentor says how long the session is and can clarify unfamiliar instructions.
- The student answers what they can. Writing “not sure,” leaving a question blank, or showing part of the reasoning are all allowed.
- Nothing about a robot or an FRC library is on the test. It covers the shell, networking, Git and Java: the things Badge 1 teaches before the Kit Bot.
- The mentor keeps the first paper. After the capstone, the student takes the test again on a fresh sheet and compares the two papers with the mentor.

</RulesBox>

### Scoring

For each question, the mentor gives one of three scores:
1. 0: the answer is incorrect or missing
2. 1: partially correct
3. 2: fully correct

Twenty points total. There is no passing mark for the pretest. The first score goes on the sign-off sheet next to *Pretest (start)*, the second next to *Pretest (end)*, and the student and mentor use both scores to plan practice. The post-test is graded: 18 of 20 (90%) or higher is expected. The mentor holds the answer key and explains missed questions after the post-test, not after the pretest.
