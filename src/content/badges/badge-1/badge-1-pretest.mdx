Take this test twice: once in your first week, before any lesson, and once after the capstone. No score is expected before you begin. The two papers help you and your mentor see what you have learned and what needs more practice.

### Pretest document

Open the [Badge 1 pretest in Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rq0OwiinRpR1t8vgwaydea3KnQgzvDeNmqIgvBGcHwo/edit) and print a copy. The document contains all ten questions and space for handwritten answers. Use a fresh copy for each attempt.

<RulesBox title="How it works">

- Ten questions on paper, without notes or internet. Write your name and date at the top. The mentor explains how long the session will be and can clarify unfamiliar instructions.
- Answer what you can. You may write “not sure,” leave a question blank, or show part of your reasoning.
- Nothing about a robot or an FRC library is on the test. It is the shell, networking, Git and Java: the things Badge 1 teaches before the Kit Bot.
- The mentor keeps the first paper. At the end, take it again on a fresh sheet and compare.

</RulesBox>

### Scoring

The mentor scores each question 0, 1 or 2: nothing or wrong, partly right, right. Twenty points total. There is no pass mark. The first score goes on the sign-off sheet next to *Pretest (start)*, the second next to *Pretest (end)*, and both scores are used to plan further practice. The mentor holds the answer key; ask for the questions you missed to be explained after the second sitting, not the first.
