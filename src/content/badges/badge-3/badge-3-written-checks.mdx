The mentor administers a diagnostic form before instruction, an independent equivalent mastery form after instruction and correction, and a separate independent final form after the capstone. Each form has twelve questions with two assessed parts each, for 24 points. Both independent completion attempts require 24 of 24. The initial diagnostic has no passing threshold and is not an entry gate. Corrections continue with the lessons; the mastery and final attempts occur after the assessed topics have been taught.

## Student Form

Use a printed or personal copy. The mentor supplies time and permits wording clarification without supplying reasoning. Independent sittings use no notes, source code, internet, or assistance. A calculator is allowed for arithmetic; show the unit conversion. The mentor changes numerical values and scenario details for equivalent forms while preserving the two assessed parts of each question.

## Corrections and Reassessment

Keep the diagnostic and review missed reasoning with the mentor using the related module. Practice may use notes and examples. After the assessed modules, complete an independent equivalent mastery attempt; do not require that result before instruction begins. The final form uses different numbers and scenarios to check understanding rather than recall of the earlier answer.

The mentor awards one point for each correct part using separate scoring guidance matched to this site revision. For question 11, accept reasoning grounded in the required FuelSim/vision world; maple-sim-specific APIs or experiments are not assessed. Record the form version. Older printed forms need this substitution before both equivalent sittings. An incomplete explanation receives feedback before another attempt. The result is recorded alongside the practical evidence; written scores do not replace hardware, integration, review or teaching demonstrations.
