The mentor administers three forms of Written Checks: a diagnostic before instruction; an
independent equivalent mastery attempt after Step 14, once the assessed topics have been taught and
the diagnostic corrected; and a separate independent final in Step 15. The diagnostic and the
mastery attempt are recorded under B3-01.1, and the final under B3-15.2. Each form has twelve
questions with two assessed parts each, for 24 points. The mastery attempt and the final each
require 24 of 24. The diagnostic has no passing threshold and is not an entry gate; corrections
continue with the lessons.

## Student Form

Use a printed or personal copy. Sittings have no time limit and are closed-notes: no notes, source
code, internet, or assistance. The mentor may clarify a question's wording without supplying
reasoning. A calculator is allowed for arithmetic; show the unit conversion. The mentor changes
numerical values and scenario details for each equivalent form while preserving the two assessed
parts of each question, so the final checks understanding rather than recall of an earlier answer.

site revision. For question 11, the mentor accepts reasoning grounded in the Step 6 launch

written scores do not replace hardware, integration, review or teaching demonstrations.
