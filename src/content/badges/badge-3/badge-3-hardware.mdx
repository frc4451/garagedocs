The student and mentor review physical evidence collected throughout the badge. Controller setup and
use begin in [Badge 2 Hardware Setup and Utilization](/badges/badge-2-hardware). Each advanced
demonstration follows its mechanism's simulation checks and the actual fixture preparation below.
The final review does not postpone physical work until after autonomous integration.

## Complete the Setup Sheet

Update the [Badge 2 fixture sheet](/badges/badge-2-hardware#complete-the-setup-sheet) for each
actual arrangement. Record configuration revision, motor/controller, bus and identity, feedback,
gearing, current/output bounds, licence requirements, reference procedure, permitted travel and
support/clearance. Add the selected output mode, gain units, profile constraints, and error,
settling, overshoot and repeatability targets for the advanced experiment.

## First Enable

Follow the [first-enable procedure](/badges/badge-2-hardware#first-enable) for a changed fixture.
Review the selected Badge 2 physical route and both CTRE simulation paths. A student with matching
physical FX evidence can reuse its voltage baseline after verifying the present configuration. A
student who used FXS or Nova physically establishes a bounded FX voltage baseline with the mentor
here. This is preparation for the experiment, not another controller-introduction lesson.

Before a torque-current comparison, show positive/reverse voltage, feedback direction, stop,
interruption and disable on the actual FX fixture. Save the configuration and physical log with
B3-06.1. For a gravity-loaded pivot, establish support, reference validity and travel limits before
normal motion. Complete characterization only within its approved effort, speed and duration bounds
and demonstrate cancellation cleanup.

## Required Demonstrations

Talon FX requires physical torque-current roller, flywheel and pivot demonstrations. On at least one
mechanism, compare voltage and torque-current control using a verified matching Badge 2 FX baseline
or a new matched voltage run. Record separate gain units and comparable conditions. Complete bounded
physical characterization with cancellation cleanup and the pivot's manual reference, absolute/fused
reference, travel and repeatability checks.

Badge 2 introduced all three controllers. Review its selected physical route as prior evidence. If
that route was FXS or Nova, matching evidence may be recorded under B3-06.2 or B3-06.3. Neither
additional physical port is required for a student who used FX; mark unselected routes as
extensions. The required advanced FX demonstrations remain B3-06.1. The FX/FXS roller, flywheel and
pivot simulation comparisons remain required and extend the earlier voltage work.

For a Nova route or extension, use the [controller introduction](/hardware/thrifty-nova-and-motors),
the actual pinned library and a documented motor/feedback arrangement. Retain configuration,
conversion, output-boundary, status and stop evidence. A general WPILib model does not emulate Nova
firmware. Cobra's `SingleRollerIONova` is a design reference to inspect, not a fixture prescription;
verify its assumptions against the selected device.

CANrange must change roller subsystem behavior during a physical presence test. The camera
demonstration in Step 9 uses stationary observations. Physical autonomous collection and scoring,
intentional jams, and injected electrical faults are not required.

## Independent Handoff

A second student follows the written setup and reference procedure with the mentor present. The
author observes and answers questions after the attempt. Correct unclear instructions and repeat the
affected action. A required hardware row remains open until its demonstration is complete. Reuse
this observed handoff in Step 13 when it meets that requirement; repeat only missing or changed
actions.

## Badge Check

S means simulation, H means hardware, W means written reasoning, and M means mentor-observed review.
The student records an evidence link, mentor signature, and date for each row. The mentor asks the
student to explain a change and its result before signing.

| Row | Work | Done When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B3-06.1 | Talon FX hardware | H/M: Verify or establish the actual FX voltage baseline, then demonstrate torque-current roller, flywheel and pivot. Compare voltage and torque-current behavior on at least one of them. |
| B3-06.2 | Talon FXS hardware | H/M: Prior-evidence review when FXS was supplied in Badge 2; otherwise an optional physical extension. Review or complete a configured physical mechanism, voltage operation, logged feedback, stop, interruption and disable. Students from the FX or Nova route may take this as an extension. |
| B3-06.3 | Thrifty Nova adaptation | H/W/M: Prior-evidence review when Nova was supplied in Badge 2; otherwise an optional physical extension. Review or complete one supported physical arrangement with verified configuration, conversion, voltage boundary, status, stop, interruption and disable. Students from the FX or FXS route may take this as an extension; no additional motor matrix is required. |
| B3-06.4 | Hardware handoff data | W/M: Record actual device IDs, bus, licences, motor, gearing, encoder, travel, limits, gains and repeatable operating procedure. Clearly distinguish fixture values from example values. |
