The student and mentor review physical evidence collected throughout the badge. Controller setup and
use begin in [Badge 2 Hardware Setup and Utilization](/badges/badge-2-hardware). Each advanced
demonstration follows its mechanism's simulation checks and the actual fixture preparation below.
The physical work happens alongside each mechanism; this step reviews it. Steps 4, 5, 7 and 10 link
here before their hardware rows, so read the setup sheet and first-enable sections before the first
powered check.

## Complete the Setup Sheet

Update the [Badge 2 fixture sheet](/badges/badge-2-hardware#complete-the-setup-sheet) for each
actual arrangement. Record configuration revision, motor/controller, bus and identity, feedback,
gearing, current/output bounds, license requirements, reference procedure, permitted travel and
support/clearance. Add the selected output mode, gain units, profile constraints, and error,
settling, overshoot and repeatability targets for the advanced experiment.

Keep a commissioning record beside the setup sheet. Record distances in inches and note the
sensor's meter reading beside each one, since the CANrange reports meters.

| Item | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Possession sensor | CANrange CAN ID and bus, mounting location, and what a detection proves about the game piece. |
| Thresholds | Occupied and empty distance ranges, signal strength in each state, and the band between them. |
| Health and freshness | Measurement health when empty and occupied, update rate, sample age, and a disconnect and reconnect log. |
| Pivot references | Manual reference procedure, absolute sensor offset, and the repeatability result. |
| Gains | Each output mode's gains with their units. |

SIM evidence does not supply these installed values. Until they are reviewed and entered, check that
the robot treats possession as unknown on hardware: a disconnected or invalid sensor reading must
stop the Step 9 readiness policy from allowing a feed.

## First Enable

Follow the [first-enable procedure](/badges/badge-2-hardware#first-enable) for a changed fixture.
Review the selected Badge 2 physical route and both CTRE simulation paths. A student with matching
physical FX evidence can reuse its voltage baseline after verifying the present configuration. A
student who used FXS or Nova physically establishes a bounded FX voltage baseline with the mentor
here, as preparation for the advanced experiments.

Before a torque-current comparison, show positive/reverse voltage, feedback direction, stop,
interruption and disable on the actual FX fixture. Save the configuration and physical log with
B3-13.1. For a gravity-loaded pivot, establish support, reference validity and travel limits before
normal motion. Complete characterization only within its approved effort, speed and duration bounds
and demonstrate cancellation cleanup.

## Required Demonstrations

`TorqueCurrentFOC` and `FusedCANcoder` feedback need a Phoenix Pro license on the device or on its
CANivore; simulation needs none. A request that passed every simulation row can produce no motion on
an unlicensed Talon FX: CTRE documents that the device disables its output, sets the
`UnlicensedFeatureInUse` fault and blinks its unlicensed pattern. Confirm license state in Phoenix
Tuner X before the appointment, and check that fault first when a torque request does nothing.

Run each demonstration through the student's own IO on the deployed robot code, not through Tuner X
controls, and make each log identify the active request type and its gain units. Before the voltage
and torque-current comparison, predict how measured speed and supply current change in each mode
when the same load is added, then compare the prediction with the log.

Row B3-13.1 requires physical torque-current roller, flywheel and pivot demonstrations. On at least
one mechanism, compare voltage and torque-current control using a verified matching Badge 2 FX
baseline or a new matched voltage run. Record separate gain units and comparable conditions.
Complete bounded physical characterization with cancellation cleanup and the pivot's manual
reference, absolute/fused reference, travel and repeatability checks.

Badge 2 introduced all three controllers. Review its selected physical route as prior evidence under
B3-13.2 or B3-13.3; the rows below say when each is an extension. The FX/FXS roller, flywheel and
pivot simulation comparisons remain required and extend the earlier voltage work.

For a Nova route or extension, use the [controller introduction](/hardware/thrifty-nova-and-motors),
the ThriftyLib version the project pins (the Starter pins 2026.0.1, `com.thethriftybot`; Step 7's
Nova absolute-reference path moves to 2026.2.2, `com.thrifty`) and a documented motor/feedback
arrangement. Retain configuration,
conversion, output-boundary, status and stop evidence. A general WPILib model does not emulate Nova
firmware. Cobra's `SingleRollerIONova` is a design reference to inspect, not a fixture prescription;
verify its assumptions against the selected device.

CANrange must change roller subsystem behavior during a physical presence test. The camera
demonstration in Step 10 uses stationary observations. Physical autonomous collection and scoring,
intentional jams, and injected electrical faults are not required.

## Independent Handoff

A second student follows the written setup and reference procedure with the mentor present. The
author observes and answers questions after the attempt. Correct unclear instructions and repeat the
affected action. The handoff supports B3-07.2, B3-13.4 and B3-15.1. A required hardware row remains
open until its demonstration is complete. Reuse
this observed handoff in Step 15 when it meets that requirement; repeat only missing or changed
actions.

## Badge Check

S means simulation, H means hardware, W means written reasoning, and M means mentor-observed review.
The student records an evidence link, an explanation in the student's own words, the mentor's
signature, and the date for each row. The mentor asks the
student to explain a change and its result before signing.

| Row | Work | Done When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B3-13.1 | Talon FX hardware | H/M: Verify or establish the actual FX voltage baseline, then demonstrate torque-current roller, flywheel and pivot. Compare voltage and torque-current behavior on at least one of them. |
| B3-13.2 | Talon FXS hardware | H/M: Prior-evidence review when FXS was supplied in Badge 2; otherwise an optional physical extension. Review or complete a configured physical mechanism, voltage operation, logged feedback, stop, interruption and disable. Students from the FX or Nova route may take this as an extension. |
| B3-13.3 | Thrifty Nova adaptation | H/W/M: Prior-evidence review when Nova was supplied in Badge 2; otherwise an optional physical extension. Review or complete one supported physical arrangement with verified configuration, conversion, voltage boundary, status, stop, interruption and disable. Students from the FX or FXS route may take this as an extension; no additional motor matrix is required. |
| B3-13.4 | Hardware handoff data | W/M: Record actual device IDs, bus, licenses, motor, gearing, encoder, travel, limits, gains and repeatable operating procedure. Distinguish fixture values from example values. |
