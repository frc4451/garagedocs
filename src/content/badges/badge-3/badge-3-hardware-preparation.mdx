Controller introduction and basic utilization now belong to [Badge 2 Hardware Setup and Utilization](/badges/badge-2-hardware). FX, FXS and Nova are taught together there. The following [software lesson](/badges/badge-2-io-and-generated-code) develops IO and configuration records, and the [roller lesson](/badges/badge-2-first-mechanism#simulation-loop) requires both CTRE vendor simulation paths.

This page preserves an earlier lesson URL; it is not a separate required Badge 3 step. Former B3-06.0 evidence can support the physical baseline reviewed under B3-06.1. Do not require the same introductory demonstration again solely because the curriculum moved.

## Bridge from Badge 2

Review the student's controller record, both CTRE simulation paths and selected physical evidence. Complete missing prerequisites with the linked Badge 2 lessons.

## Complete the Setup Sheet

Use the [Badge 2 fixture sheet](/badges/badge-2-hardware#complete-the-setup-sheet) and add the [advanced experiment settings](/badges/badge-3-hardware#complete-the-setup-sheet).

## First Enable

Follow the [Badge 3 fixture check](/badges/badge-3-hardware#first-enable) before advanced physical operation.

## Establish a Voltage Baseline

Reuse matching physical FX evidence or establish a baseline on the actual fixture before its voltage/current comparison. Simulation evidence does not substitute for this physical check.
