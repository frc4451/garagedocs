Badge 3: Advanced FRC is the Automation Team capstone. The student refines mechanisms, integrates sensing and autonomous behavior, explains test evidence, and helps another member succeed. The mentor assesses technical ownership and readiness for leadership.

Every student receives the same versioned `Badge3RobotBase` and makes assessed changes in `Badge3Robot`. Badge 2 or mentor-verified equivalent work is the prerequisite. Supplied code establishes the starting point; the student explains it and demonstrates the additions below.

The table gives the main learning sequence. Review the Badge 2 hardware evidence at entry; each advanced physical demonstration follows its mechanism's simulation checks. Calculus and simulation may continue while hardware appointments are pending. FuelSim and pivot work can proceed alongside one another. Object-detection geometry can begin from the Badge 2 vision prerequisites; autonomous integration waits for mechanisms, states, observations and FuelSim. Leadership evidence accumulates throughout.

[Exploring maple-sim](/badges/badge-3-maple) is an optional specialization after the autonomous capstone. It adds contact-model investigation without becoming a second required scoring world.

## Evidence and Support

Assessment identifiers such as B3-02.1 remain stable when lessons move; they identify outcomes rather than current step numbers. Use the [curriculum revision record](/badges/badge-3-sign-off#curriculum-revision-record) to reconcile earlier work. The site rows govern the revised path; Google Docs masters remain unchanged.

Every required row needs an evidence link, an explanation in the student's own words, a mentor signature, and a date. Define performance targets before tuning and retain successful and failed runs. A live visualization does not replace a saved log.

The first written check diagnoses gaps without blocking instruction. Corrections continue with the lessons. After the assessed topics, complete an independent 100% mastery attempt, then a separate independent 100% final. Worksheet reasoning is corrected to 100% with feedback. Completed practical evidence remains credited. See [Written Checks](/badges/badge-3-written-checks).

Controller introduction now belongs to Badge 2. Talon FX hardware remains provided for advanced work. A verified physical voltage baseline, FX roller/flywheel/pivot work, voltage/current comparison, characterization, CANrange, pivot references and stationary camera checks remain required. Credit matching Badge 2 FX, FXS or Nova introductory evidence. FXS/Nova review rows apply only to the selected prior route; extra physical vendor ports are optional. The advanced FX/FXS simulation comparisons remain required. Autonomous collection and scoring stay in simulation. Intentional physical jams, automatic retry, QuestNav, Choreo, SystemCore and SPARK MAX instruction are outside this required path.

## Calculus for Controls

The existing [Calculus for Controls](/badges/badge-3-calculus) module keeps identifiers C1–C5 and the current worksheet. Earlier completed work may count after mentor review. Its calculations become evidence for B3-02.1.
