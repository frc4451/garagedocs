Badge 3: Advanced FRC is the Automation Team's third-year badge. Its requirements are not settled, so this page holds what it is expected to certify and where its material will come from. Nothing on it is a sign-off row yet.

### Expected to certify

A third-year member who holds Badge 3 is expected to lead a subsystem from design to competition: own its code from the IO layer up, characterise and tune it, write its autonomous behaviour, review other members' pull requests, and hand it to the next year with a lesson written for this site.

### What exists today

The technical half is spread across the top of the [FRC](/frc) section: [Cobra Architecture](/frc/virtual-subsystem) with [VARC](/frc/varc), [State-Driven Robot Code](/frc/why-state-machines), [Autonomous](/frc/pathplanner), the [Mechanism Examples](/frc/yamg), and [Unit Tests in Simulation](/frc/unit-tests-in-simulation). The leadership half (reviewing pull requests, mentoring a first-year through Badge 1, writing a lesson) has no pages yet and is the part the mentors have to define.

### Before it is written

The badge follows the same shape as [Badge 1](/badges/badge-1): a pretest at the start and the end, numbered steps that list existing lessons with a *done when* per row, badge checks a mentor can see, and a sign-off sheet. When the mentors settle the outcomes, this page becomes the badge's *What It Certifies* page and the steps follow it in this list.
