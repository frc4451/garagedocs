### Separate power from signals

Turn off the robot and disconnect the battery before changing wiring. Power conductors carry enough current to heat a loose connection. Signal conductors carry measurements or network traffic and can fail without an obvious visual sign.

Use the current FRC wiring diagram and inspection rules when selecting wire, breakers, fuses, connectors, and branch circuits; [Wire Gauge and Selection](/hardware/wire-gauge-and-selection) explains how the breaker on a channel sets the wire it needs. A vendor device's maximum rating does not replace the FRC rules for the current season.

### Build connections that survive motion

- Strip only the length required by the terminal.
- Keep all strands inside the connector.
- Tug-test each power conductor after insertion.
- Add strain relief so vibration does not pull on a terminal or port.
- Label both ends of power, CAN, and Ethernet cables.
- Leave service loops where a device must be removed for repair.
- Protect exposed circuit boards from chips, fasteners, and conductive debris.

### Wire CAN as one bus

CAN uses a twisted yellow and green pair. Each device joins the same bus and needs a unique ID. The bus needs termination at both ends. Avoid branches and star connections unless the installed hardware and current rules explicitly support that topology.

With robot power off, measure resistance between CAN High and CAN Low at an accessible connector. A normally terminated bus is about `60 ohms`, because two `120 ohm` terminators are connected in parallel. Treat a different reading as evidence to inspect the topology and terminations. Do not use a resistance check on a powered bus.

### Document the robot

Maintain one hardware table beside the code:

| Device label | Vendor and model | CAN ID or address | Bus | Power branch | Code constant |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Example: left drive front | Talon FX | 1 | `rio` | PDH 0, 40 A | `FRONT_LEFT_DRIVE_ID` |

The physical label, vendor client, wiring table, and code constant should agree. Update all four when a device moves.

<RulesBox title="Done when">

Produce a labeled wiring photo and device table for one robot. With power off, tug-test the critical power connections and record the CAN resistance. A mentor confirms the power protection and wiring against the current rules.

</RulesBox>
