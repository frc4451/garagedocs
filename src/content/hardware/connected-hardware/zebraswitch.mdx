### What ZebraSwitch means here

**ZebraSwitch** is Team 900's documented FRC approach for adding a compact Ethernet switch near the radio and coprocessors. It is a team-developed assembly and wiring practice, not a vendor product named ZebraSwitch. FRC 4451 has used this approach, so GarageDocs preserves the name and the checks needed to maintain it.

The team's installed ZebraSwitch is treated as a **5 V powered mini switch**. Verify the exact switch board and regulator on the robot before replacing either part. Team 900's newer paper and current commercial switch revisions may accept a wider input range, while an older or custom assembly may still require regulated 5 V.

### Why the robot needs a switch

An Ethernet switch adds wired ports for a roboRIO, PhotonVision coprocessor, QuestNav adapter, and service laptop. It forwards Ethernet frames automatically and does not receive a CAN ID or robot-code configuration.

```text
VH-109 radio -> ZebraSwitch -> roboRIO
                          -> PhotonVision coprocessor
                          -> QuestNav Ethernet adapter
                          -> service connection
```

### Inspect the installed assembly

1. Turn off the robot and disconnect the battery.
2. Read the switch-board label and regulator part number.
3. Mark the regulator input and the switch's 5 V input. Do not assume the connectors protect against reversed polarity.
4. Trace every Ethernet cable and label both ends.
5. Inspect the enclosure for conductive debris and cable strain.
6. Reconnect power and verify input voltage before attaching the switch.
7. Connect one Ethernet device at a time and record which link indicator appears.

The switch has no configuration client. Use link indicators, `ping`, each endpoint's web interface, and the Driver Station to test the network from the physical layer upward.

<RulesBox title="Done when">

The robot wiring document names the exact switch board and regulator. The installed mini switch receives verified 5 V power, every Ethernet cable is labeled, and the roboRIO and each coprocessor remain reachable while the robot moves.

</RulesBox>
