### The robot network bridge

The Vivid-Hosting VH-109 connects the robot Ethernet network to the Driver Station. In the shop, one VH-109 can run as the robot radio while another runs as the access point. At an event, field staff configure the radio for the field.

### Power and ports

Use the current FRC wiring diagram. WPILib recommends redundant radio power through the direct power input and the supported Power over Ethernet path when that wiring is allowed. Secure the power and Ethernet cables so robot impacts do not load the connectors.

The radio has several Ethernet ports. Record which port connects to the roboRIO, each coprocessor or switch, and any service connector. A link light proves a physical Ethernet connection; it does not prove that team settings or IP addressing are correct.

### Configuration interface

1. Connect a computer to the documented configuration port.
2. Open `http://radio.local/`.
3. Record the current firmware and mode before changing anything.
4. Verify the downloaded firmware checksum against the Vivid-Hosting release page.
5. Update firmware without removing power.
6. Choose robot-radio or access-point mode.
7. Apply the approved team number, suffix, and keys.
8. Do not publish team radio passwords in screenshots, issues, or repositories.

After configuration, prove the whole path with the Driver Station and `ping`. Follow [R00 Part 3](/kit-bot/r00-radio-setup) for the team-number example and shop sequence.

<RulesBox title="Done when">

The radio reports approved firmware and the intended mode. The Driver Station reaches the roboRIO through the radio, and the team has a private record of the settings needed to rebuild the configuration.

</RulesBox>
