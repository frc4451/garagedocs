### Why the team uses a Quest 3S

QuestNav turns the Meta Quest 3S headset's visual-inertial tracking into robot pose data. QuestNav recommends the Quest 3S for FRC use. The headset remains a consumer device, so reliable robot use depends on setup, mounting, charging, cable retention, and a repeatable match checklist.

### Prepare the headset

1. Complete the normal Meta headset setup.
2. Create and verify the required Meta developer account.
3. Enable Developer Mode in the Meta Horizon app.
4. Connect a USB-C data cable and authorize USB debugging.
5. Open the [QuestNav Setup Tool](https://setup.questnav.gg/).
6. Apply the FRC profile and install both QuestNav and QuestNav KeepAwake.
7. Reboot and verify that the applications start as documented.

KeepAwake matters because Quest 3 and Quest 3S sleep behavior cannot be fully disabled through their normal Android settings.

### Mount and connect it

Use a rigid mount designed for the Quest 3S. Keep the tracking cameras clear and provide a service loop and strain relief at USB-C. The Quest 3S pose origin differs from the Quest 3, so use the QuestNav mounting diagram for the exact headset.

Connect the supported USB-C Ethernet and power adapter. QuestNav troubleshooting recommends verifying that the power path can provide the required 5 V current and that headset charge stays level or rises during operation. Record the exact adapter, power source, and cable used by the robot.

### Pre-match evidence

- QuestNav and QuestNavLib versions match.
- Wi-Fi is disabled when the wired network is required.
- Ethernet reports connected.
- KeepAwake is running.
- Battery percentage is stable or increasing.
- Tracking is valid and the pose responds to robot motion.
- The mount and cable retention pass a shake test.

Continue to [QuestNav](/frc/questnav) for coordinate frames, pose resets, and fusion with the drivetrain estimator.

<RulesBox title="Done when">

The Quest 3S is configured by the setup tool, rigidly mounted with clear camera views, powered and networked through the documented adapter, and reporting a stable pose. The team can repeat the pre-match check without removing the headset.

</RulesBox>
